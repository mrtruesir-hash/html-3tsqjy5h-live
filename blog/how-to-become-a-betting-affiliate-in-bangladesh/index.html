Step 1: Pick a Cricket or Casino Niche That Fits Bangladesh Demand

Your first decision is what kind of player you want to attract, because that choice shapes every piece of content you produce afterward. In Bangladesh, cricket is the dominant sport by a wide margin, and betting interest rises and falls with the calendar of the Bangladesh Premier League, the national team fixtures, and major ICC tournaments. A cricket-focused niche gives you predictable seasonal demand: audiences actively search for match previews, team form, and odds comparisons in the days before a big game. If you enjoy following the sport and can produce timely content, cricket is the most natural entry point for a new affiliate targeting this market, and it aligns closely with how local players already think about betting.

The casino niche is the second major lane, and it behaves very differently from sports. Casino interest is not tied to a fixture list, so it produces steadier, year-round demand for content around slots, live dealer tables, and game reviews. This makes casino a useful complement to cricket rather than a competitor: a cricket audience goes quiet between tournaments, while casino content keeps earning in the gaps. Many experienced affiliates in Bangladesh run a hybrid content mix, leaning into cricket during BPL and ICC windows and pushing casino offers during the off-season to smooth out their monthly revenue.

Within either lane, resist the urge to cover everything at once. A focused niche such as BPL match content, or a single casino vertical like live dealer games, lets you rank and build authority faster than a generic betting site would. Depth signals expertise to both search engines and readers, and it makes your recommendations more credible. You can always expand once one niche is producing conversions. For a broader view of how these niches map to real programs and payouts, our pillar on iGaming affiliate programs in Bangladesh is worth reading alongside this step.

Whatever niche you choose, frame your work as recruitment and information for adults who already gamble, not as encouragement to start. Online betting is legally restricted in Bangladesh under the Public Gambling Act of 1867, and players reach operators through offshore platforms. As an affiliate you are marketing to an existing audience, so keep your content responsible, age-gated where possible, and free of promises about outcomes. This is affiliate awareness, not legal advice, and treating it professionally protects your business as it grows.

Step 2: Choose a Traffic Channel Suited to the Local Audience

The Bangladesh audience is mobile-first, heavily Android, and price-sensitive, which should directly influence where you invest your effort. Search engine optimisation is the most durable channel: a well-structured site that ranks for cricket previews or casino game reviews earns traffic continuously without paying per click. SEO rewards patience and consistency, so it suits affiliates who are willing to publish steadily over months. Because the audience browses on phones, your pages must load fast on modest data connections and read cleanly on small screens; a slow or cluttered site loses Bangladeshi visitors before they ever reach your link.

Telegram communities are the second channel that fits this market especially well. Telegram is widely used for sports discussion, tips, and match chatter, and it lets you build a direct, repeat audience you own rather than rent. A disciplined channel that posts match context, responsible reminders, and clearly labelled affiliate links can convert well during cricket season because members are already engaged and returning daily. The key is to provide genuine value between promotions so the channel does not read as pure advertising, which erodes trust and engagement quickly.

Short-form and long-form video round out the mix, and they play to Bangladesh's mobile viewing habits. YouTube supports deeper content such as match analysis and casino game walkthroughs, while short vertical video captures attention fast and travels well across social feeds. Video also lets you demonstrate credibility with a face and a voice, which builds trust faster than text alone. You do not need expensive equipment; a phone, a clear script, and consistency matter far more than production polish for this audience.

Whichever channels you pick, tie them to the cricket calendar to maximise return. Publishing and promoting heavily around BPL, national team matches, and ICC tournaments concentrates your effort where demand spikes, and it lets a small operation punch above its weight. Once you can generate links, decide how you want to be paid, because the commission model shapes your whole strategy. RevShare pays you an ongoing percentage of the net revenue generated by the players you refer, for as long as they keep playing. This rewards quality and loyalty: if you attract engaged cricket and casino players who stay active across seasons, RevShare can compound into a substantial recurring income stream over time.

CPA, by contrast, pays a fixed amount for each qualifying player you refer, up to 110 US dollars per conversion. This model gives you predictable, immediate returns per signup, which suits high-volume channels where you can drive many first-time depositors quickly, such as a large Telegram community or a viral video during a major tournament. CPA is often the more comfortable choice for beginners because you see a clear cash figure per conversion and can calculate your earnings without waiting to see how players behave over the following months.

The hybrid deal blends the two, giving you an upfront CPA payment plus a share of ongoing revenue. This is frequently the most balanced option once you have some data, because it smooths out the trade-off: you get immediate cash flow to reinvest in content and traffic, while still building a long-term RevShare tail from the players who stick around. As your understanding of your audience deepens, a hybrid arrangement lets you capture both the quick win and the compounding upside without committing entirely to one or the other.

Your best choice depends on the players your channel produces, not on which number looks biggest. High-value, long-retention players favour RevShare; high-volume, lower-retention traffic favours CPA; most maturing affiliates land on hybrid. Review your dashboard data after a few weeks and match the model to reality rather than guessing at the start. Once you identify a winner, do more of it: produce similar content, promote it harder, and cut or rework the placements that generate traffic but no conversions.
