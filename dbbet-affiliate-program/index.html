The DBBET Affiliate Program: A Complete Guide

By Ray Truelead, Head of Affiliates · Last verified: 2026-08-19

DBBET Partners is the affiliate program for the DBBET sportsbook and casino brand. This guide brings together, in one place, everything DBBET Partners itself publishes about how the program works — commission models, tracking, payments, requirements and the application process — so you can evaluate it without digging through multiple pages. Where a specific figure isn't something we can independently confirm as current, we say so rather than guessing.

What is the DBBET affiliate program?

It's a referral/affiliate marketing program: you promote DBBET's sportsbook and casino product using your own tracking links, and you earn a commission based on the players you refer and how they play. dbbetaff.com (this site) is an independent affiliate resource covering the program — it is not the official DBBET operator or the official DBBET Partners programme website. See our About page for the full relationship. If you're new to iGaming affiliate marketing generally, start with our iGaming Affiliate Marketing guide before diving into DBBET specifics.

How the program works

You register for a DBBET Partners account, get approved, and receive a unique referral link (plus promo codes and marketing materials). When someone signs up and plays through your link, that activity is attributed to your account, and you earn commission according to the model you're on.

Who can become an affiliate

The program is aimed at marketers, webmasters, media buyers and other traffic owners — anyone with an audience or traffic source that can be directed toward a sportsbook/casino offer, subject to DBBET's own approval and eligibility checks.

Commission models

CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)

A fixed payment for each qualifying player you refer. DBBET Partners' own published figure is up to $110 per player — this is a stated maximum, not a flat rate every affiliate receives; the exact figure Ray confirmed is "up to $110, not exact," so treat it as a ceiling rather than a guaranteed number.

Revenue Share (RevShare)

An ongoing share of the net revenue generated by the players you refer, for as long as they keep playing. DBBET Partners' published, confirmed figure is up to 55%.

Hybrid

A combination of both: a fixed CPA payment when a player first qualifies, plus an ongoing Revenue Share on their long-term activity. This balances upfront cash flow with recurring income.

Terms may vary and should be confirmed with the affiliate team before you commit a campaign to a specific model. For the general mechanics and a break-even calculation between these three models, see CPA vs RevShare vs Hybrid and What Is RevShare?, or estimate your own numbers with our CPA vs RevShare Calculator.

Tracking

Referral links use long-duration tracking, so a player who signs up later is still attributed to your account. Tracking is described by DBBET Partners as real-time and visible in your own statistics dashboard — the exact cookie/attribution window length isn't published anywhere we can independently confirm, so ask your affiliate manager if that specific detail matters for your campaign planning.

Reporting

Your dashboard shows current balance, pending amount and payout history, along with click, registration and deposit statistics tied to your links.

Traffic sources

DBBET Partners' own guidance lists Telegram channels, review sites, social media, paid campaigns and tipster communities as accepted traffic sources. If you're unsure whether your specific traffic source qualifies, confirm with your affiliate manager before investing in it.

Promotional materials

Referral links, promo codes and marketing materials are made available through the affiliate dashboard once your account is approved. Specific creative formats and sizes aren't published anywhere we can verify independently — check what's available directly in your dashboard after signing up.

GEO availability

DBBET Partners' own marketing describes a focus on Asia, Africa, CIS, the Middle East and LatAm, and the DBBET Partners blog publishes country-specific guidance for several markets, including Bangladesh, Tanzania, Sri Lanka, Somalia, Uzbekistan, Azerbaijan and the wider CIS region — real evidence of where the program is actively promoted, though not necessarily an exhaustive or exclusive list. We do not have a verified, complete list of accepted or restricted countries. Confirm eligibility for your specific GEO directly with the affiliate team before building a campaign around it.

Payment information

Per DBBET Partners' own published terms, affiliates can choose between two payout schedules:

  • Weekly payouts, with a $30 minimum.
  • Monthly payouts, with a $100 minimum.

Supported payment methods described by DBBET Partners include bank transfer, popular e-wallets and cryptocurrency such as Bitcoin and USDT. Availability of specific methods can vary by region — confirm what's available to you with your affiliate manager.

Requirements

You need a real traffic source or audience you can direct toward the offer, and you'll go through DBBET's own approval process when you register. We don't have a verified list of minimum-traffic thresholds or other hard requirements beyond this — terms may vary and should be confirmed with the affiliate team.

Restrictions

As with most iGaming affiliate programs, specific traffic sources or geos may be restricted or require case-by-case approval. DBBET Partners' own FAQ content advises contacting your affiliate manager if you're unsure whether your geo or traffic source is eligible — we don't have a published, exhaustive restrictions list to reproduce here.

Application process

  1. Register for a DBBET Partners account.
  2. Get approved (DBBET's own review process).
  3. Access your referral links, promo codes and marketing materials from the dashboard.
  4. Share them through your traffic source and track performance in real time.

Advantages

  • Choice of commission model (CPA, RevShare or Hybrid) rather than one fixed structure.
  • A weekly payout option with a relatively low $30 minimum, useful if you want faster cash flow than a typical monthly-only program.
  • Real-time tracking and a self-serve dashboard for stats and payout history.
  • Active content/guidance published for several specific emerging markets (see our blog), which can help you understand a market before committing traffic to it.

Limitations

To be balanced rather than promotional: DBBET is a smaller, more emerging-market-focused brand than some of the largest global sportsbook affiliate programs, which sometimes advertise higher headline commission ceilings. Our own DBBET Partners vs Mostbet Partners comparison covers this trade-off directly — a program with a lower headline rate can still out-earn a higher-rate one if it converts and retains your specific traffic better, but that depends on your traffic, not the headline number alone. We'd rather point that out than only show the upside.

FAQ

How much can I earn with the DBBET affiliate program?

Earnings depend on the quality of your traffic, the activity and value of the players you refer, and the commission model you choose. Active, high-deposit players on a Revenue Share model can generate recurring income, while CPA pays a fixed amount per qualifying player. Ask your affiliate manager for guidance based on your specific traffic before relying on headline percentages alone.

Can I combine CPA and Revenue Share?

Yes. The Hybrid model combines a fixed CPA payment for each qualifying player with an ongoing Revenue Share on their long-term activity. Ask your affiliate manager to tailor the right mix for your traffic.

How and when do affiliates get paid?

Per the official DBBET Partners terms, affiliates can choose weekly payouts (30 USD minimum) or monthly payouts (100 USD minimum). Supported payment methods include bank transfer, popular e-wallets and cryptocurrency such as Bitcoin and USDT. Confirm current options with your affiliate manager, as available methods can vary by region.

Which countries and traffic sources are accepted?
