iGaming Affiliate Commission Benchmark 2026

By Ray Truelead, Head of Affiliates · Published 2026-08-19 · Last updated 2026-08-19 · Data verification date: 2026-08-19

Most public "commission comparison" content in this niche restates marketing copy without saying where a number actually came from or when it was last checked. This page does the opposite: every figure below states its source, the date it was checked, and one of four explicit data-quality labels. Where we couldn't verify a number, we say so instead of guessing.

Methodology

We classify every data point into one of four categories:

  1. Verified first-party — confirmed directly by the program's own team or the program's own published terms.
  2. Publicly available — stated on the program's own public-facing marketing page, but not independently confirmed by the program's team.
  3. Third-party reported — reported by affiliate-program aggregators/review sites rather than the operator itself; treated as directional, not authoritative.
  4. Requires verification — no reliable figure found; the field is left blank or marked "Not publicly disclosed" rather than estimated.

For DBBET Partners, figures are sourced from direct confirmation by DBBET's own affiliate team and DBBET's own published terms (see our DBBET Affiliate Program page). We have no first-party access to any other program listed here, so every other row is sourced from third-party aggregator sites, cited individually below the table. Where different sources reported meaningfully different numbers for the same program, we show the reported range and flag the disagreement rather than pick one figure — that disagreement is itself a real, useful data point about how opaque these programs' public terms are.

Definitions

CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)

A fixed, one-time payment for each qualifying referred player, usually triggered by a first deposit. See What Is an iGaming Affiliate Program?

RevShare (Revenue Share)

An ongoing percentage of a referred player's net gaming revenue, paid for as long as they stay active. See What Is RevShare?

Hybrid

A combination of a (usually smaller) upfront CPA plus an ongoing (usually reduced) RevShare on the same player. See CPA vs RevShare vs Hybrid.

Limitations

  • We had first-party access to DBBET's own terms only. Every other program's figures come from third-party aggregators, not the operator directly — several of those aggregators' own numbers disagreed with each other, which we've shown as a range rather than resolved by guessing.
  • Two operators' official affiliate domains (Parimatch, Royal Partners) blocked automated access, so we could not independently confirm their aggregator-reported figures against the operator's own page.
  • iGaming affiliate commission rates are typically negotiated per affiliate based on traffic volume, quality and GEO — the figures shown are headline/maximum rates, not what any specific affiliate is guaranteed to receive.
  • This benchmark currently covers four programs. We'll expand it over time as we're able to independently verify more programs' terms, rather than padding the table with unverified rows.

Methodology notes

Affiliate terms in this industry change frequently and are rarely published as a single authoritative figure — this is precisely why every row above carries its own "Date checked" rather than one blanket update date for the page. If you're citing this page, please cite the specific row's date and source rather than the page's general "Last updated" date, since different rows were verified through different means and carry different confidence levels.

FAQ

Why do sources disagree on some programs' rates?

Most iGaming affiliate programs negotiate rates individually by GEO, traffic volume and quality, and rarely publish one single official number. Third-party aggregators often report different figures because they're citing different negotiated deals or different points in time. Where our sources disagreed significantly, we show the range and flag it rather than pick one number.

Is DBBET's data on this page more reliable than the other programs' data?

For DBBET, we used first-party confirmation directly from DBBET's own affiliate team and published terms, which is why it's labeled verified first-party. For every other program, we relied on third-party aggregator sources because we had no first-party access to their programs — this is stated plainly in the table's Data Type column for every row.

How often is this benchmark updated?

Affiliate commission terms change frequently and are often renegotiated per affiliate. We recommend treating the Date Checked column as the authoritative freshness signal and confirming any figure directly with the program in question before relying on it.
